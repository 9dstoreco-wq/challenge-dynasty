CHALLENGE DYNASTY — VERCEL ENV

In Vercel Project Settings > Environment Variables, configure for Production and Preview:
NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL=<Supabase project URL>
NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=<Supabase anon/public key>

Do not put the service_role key in NEXT_PUBLIC_* variables.
After saving, redeploy.
